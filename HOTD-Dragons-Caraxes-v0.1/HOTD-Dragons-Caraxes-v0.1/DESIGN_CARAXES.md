# Caraxes design target

## Silhouette

Caraxes should be recognizable from distance by silhouette alone.

- Extremely long, narrow neck.
- Extremely long tail.
- Small, relatively narrow torso compared with overall length.
- Long, narrow wings rather than a broad heavy-dragon wing plan.
- Slender, aggressive head profile.
- Backward-pointing horn structure.
- Dorsal spines should form a broken, uneven ridge rather than a clean saw-tooth line.

The visual target is a dragon that reads as a flying serpent, not a standard quadruped scaled longer.

## Palette

Prototype palette:
- primary scales: deep blood red
- shadow scales / dorsal ridge: near-black burgundy
- wing membranes: darker desaturated crimson
- eyes / small accents: amber-gold
- scars / worn scale patches: muted brown-red

Avoid high saturation. Caraxes should look weathered, dangerous and organic rather than toy-like.

## Movement language

Ground:
- body should carry a slight lateral serpentine motion
- neck should lead turns before the torso
- tail should lag behind turns

Flight:
- strong banking
- sharper turning radius than very large dragons
- long neck and tail should trail the body's angular momentum
- body should have subtle wave motion during sustained flight
- dive attacks should feel fast and predatory

## Gameplay identity

Caraxes is not the heaviest dragon.

Strengths:
- high maneuverability
- strong acceleration
- sharp turns
- dangerous sustained fire pass
- aggressive melee pressure

Weaknesses:
- less raw durability than giant ancient dragons
- narrower fire coverage
- vulnerable if caught in prolonged direct grappling against much heavier dragons

## Scale target

Final visual length should feel enormous without making normal terrain impossible to navigate.
A practical adult target is roughly 14-18 Minecraft blocks nose-to-tail before animation stretch,
subject to server performance and collision testing.

## v0.1 blockout

The included GeckoLib model is intentionally rough.
Its job is to validate:
- bone hierarchy
- renderer registration
- animation file routing
- scale perception
- rider / entity test loop

The detailed model should only be locked after the first in-game test.
