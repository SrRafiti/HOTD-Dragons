# Third-party notes

## Saint's Dragons

This prototype is designed as an addon that requires Saint's Dragons to be installed.

The addon does **not** redistribute Saint's Dragons models, textures, animations or sounds.
Its Caraxes geometry, texture and animation resources are newly created for this prototype.

Before public release, verify the current Saint's Dragons license terms and attribution
requirements for the exact version being targeted.
